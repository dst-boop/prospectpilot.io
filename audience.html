    Plain-text plan

    Review in ZoomInfo before paying for an export

    Look at a small sample: are the employer, role and office location relevant? Treat missing or conflicting details as review items, not proof. There is no pre-upload lead preview in this guide.

    Check credits and firm approval before exporting. Exports can spend credits; your admin may restrict them. Use the arrow beside Export and choose Excel for a CSV. The main button may send to the last destination instead.

    Official export instructions ↗

    Optional: test an exclusion

    Compare a small sample with and without the proposed exclusion. Note relevant people kept, irrelevant people removed, and relevant people missed. Use that tradeoff to decide, not result count alone. Review manually if your edition has no documented exclusion control; do not assume Boolean NOT syntax.

    Record sample size and relevance criteria. A missing source or empty result is not proof nobody fits. Permission and DNC safeguards are never optional exclusions.

    Optional: brainstorm a conversation opener

    Only use a dated professional event you have checked against the person. A company event alone does not establish an individual layoff or a need for advice. Leave this blank if evidence is missing.

    After your ZoomInfo review: import a CSV

    Use the existing CSV import in Contacts. Preview the file first; that preview does not save contacts. Check rejected rows, identities, source dates and direct/mobile restrictions before choosing Import contacts.

    Search quality comes from review, not volume. After upload, review which records actually fit. Come back here to adjust your next search. This guide does not automatically learn from or change uploaded records.
