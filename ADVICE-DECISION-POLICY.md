# ProspectPilot advice-decision policy

The pilot’s objective is to sign new client households within 90 days of intake. Use recorded decision intent, a feasible next step and transparent care with contact information to choose where advisors spend their time.

## Research and decisions

## Operating rules

1. **Discovery.** Begin with requests for advice, accepted introductions and firm-approved educational outreach. For cold research, use public professional announcements, the DOL Form 5500 catalog, state WARN and licensed sources. Form 5500 and WARN inform employer selection. Person-level reviews require a dated source that connects the event to the individual. Family, estate and similar private topics enter the worklist through voluntary disclosures. [DOL Form 5500 datasets](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/ebsa/about-ebsa/our-activities/public-disclosure/foia/form-5500-datasets), [New York WARN](https://dol.ny.gov/warn-dashboard)
2. **Qualification.** A reviewed request, accepted introduction or conversation must establish a specific planning decision for the person. Review US residence and a direct contact route separately. Suppressed or ambiguous records lose contact shortcuts. Age estimates, titles, net worth, plan averages and balances have zero weight in the active qualification rule. Approved service minimums can be addressed when matching services and disclosing fees.
3. **Priority.** Work through due commitments and booked meetings first, then requests, accepted introductions, confirmed decisions with a date within 30 days, other first-party decisions and reviewed public events. Recent requests that need contact verification remain near the top as review tasks. Enter actual dates supplied by the person. A date that has passed requires reconfirmation. Contact reviews, decision reviews and public event signals use 90-day windows. The 30-day and 90-day cutoffs are pilot settings chosen for review and measurement.
4. **Verification and cost.** Record who supplied or reviewed the evidence, when it was observed, the direct route and the reason it is usable. Apply opt-out and number restrictions before offering an action. Use free public and first-party evidence before spending. The Lab’s provider export requires reviewed residence, a recent person-level public event, matching professional identifiers and a missing direct route. Existing provider allowances apply independently. [FINRA Rule 3230](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/3230), [FTC data protection guide](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/protecting-personal-information-guide-business)
5. **Inbound.** Pilot under the participating firm’s approved domain, with a specific educational page and a separate invitation to request help. Start with “What are my options for an old workplace retirement account?” Give the consumer free access to the resource and let them choose whether to request contact, the channel and the appropriate time. Show the firm and who will receive the request. The advisor can record these inquiries in the workspace. Direct web intake, paid referrals and a consumer marketplace each need a separate implementation. [Investor.gov registration and relationship summaries](https://www.investor.gov/CRS), [SEC lead-generation and referral observations](https://www.sec.gov/compliance/risk-alerts/additional-observations-regarding-advisers-compliance-advisers-act-marketing-rule)

## Pilot measurement

Track 30-, 60- and 90-day acquisition cohorts by original source and advisor. The primary outcome is a signed engagement for a unique new household. Record signing dates and suppress duplicate households when measuring it. Include all enrolled advisors in signed households per advisor, and include advisor time, verification, subscriptions and campaign spend in cost per signed household. Compare event-triggered outreach with ordinary permitted outreach inside the same advisor group, with similar follow-up effort. Report inbound and accepted referrals as their own cohorts. Track time to first response, meeting held, signing and opt-outs to diagnose the result. Existing workspace milestones use the dates advisors logged outcomes and count records. Those milestones are available during the pilot. Actual signing dates and deduplicated household reporting need further instrumentation.
