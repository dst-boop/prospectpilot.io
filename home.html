Before you start

A few practical answers.

Does ProspectPilot include a contact database?

No. Start with contacts you are authorized to use, such as your ZoomInfo export. Optional provider search and enrichment require a configured provider account and budget. Public research coverage varies.

What happens after I sign in?

Import contacts or choose existing contacts from your directory. Your worklist points to follow-ups and evidence reviews. Open a prospect to see the brief, review details, and record the next step.

Will it email or call people for me?

No. The app prepares drafts and organizes follow-ups. You decide whether to contact someone and use your approved email or calling tools. Activity is counted when you log it.

What is the advisor evidence review?

The advisor workspace separates reviewed evidence from unknown information. Personal financial facts require disclosure or authorized documents; a job title or employer does not establish an individual’s wealth or retirement assets.
