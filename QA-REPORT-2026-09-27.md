# ProspectPilot workflow and data-quality audit — 27 September 2026

## Assessment

The live app supports an authorized CSV-to-worklist workflow, but the complete discovery → qualified lead → enrichment → pipeline goal is not yet demonstrated. Import, replay protection, free job execution, worklist handoff, saved follow-ups, closure, and do-not-contact propagation passed with two explicitly fictional records. Paid enrichment and automated company-first discovery are not operationally validated.

The highest-value next release is the tested current application, followed by a guided discovery/import → review → enrichment → next-action flow. More imported rows alone will not produce better qualified leads.

## Fixes included with this audit

1. Require a strong person-specific identifier before automatic identity confirmation. Names, titles, employer context, generic mailboxes and shared business phones cannot independently confirm a person.
2. Preserve distinct email punctuation; normalize only valid US-format phone identifiers; reject international-number suffix collisions and malformed plus placement. Canonicalize LinkedIn profile URLs while routing conflicting profiles to review.
3. Prevent repeated imports from manufacturing confidence or a verified badge. Inferred and unsupported exact ages are not described as verified.
4. Preserve explicit conflicts, present conflicting ages as unresolved ranges, withhold age qualification points for conflicts, and ignore conflicting graduation/career dates for age inference.
5. Clear terminal records from enrichment selection, including when a save finishes after the user has opened another prospect.

These are source changes pending review/deployment. Identity/evidence fixes protect the new V1 helper modules before integration; the selection change affects the existing advisor UI. No database migrations, provider calls, recorded enrichment resets or suppression resets are included.
